# Image credits

Every photograph on this site, where it came from and under what licence. `scripts/ingest-image.mjs`
appends a row to the table below for each photograph it brings in, so keep the table the last
thing in this file.

## Temporary stock photography

Every row dated 2026-11-08 is a **temporary stock image**, standing in until 4Jasmine Dr supplies its
own photographs. None of them shows 4Jasmine Dr's vehicles, premises or people, and their alt text
says only what is in the picture. HANDOVER.md section 4 explains what to undo when the client's
photographs land: replace the file, rewrite its alt text, and delete its row here.

All of them are public domain — CC0 1.0 or the Public Domain Mark — so they may be used
commercially with no credit on the site. The photographer is recorded anyway. They came from
StockSnap and from Wikimedia Commons (searched through Openverse); none was taken from a search
engine, a paid library or another company's website.

Slots used by live pages that were left empty, because no suitable public-domain photograph was
found, keep their branded placeholder:

- `images/fleet/fleet-delivery-vehicle.jpg` — no public-domain photograph of a modern light delivery van that did not carry another company's name
- `images/gallery/gallery-morning-dispatch.jpg` — no public-domain photograph of several trucks parked together, large enough to use
- `images/gallery/gallery-load-check.jpg` — no public-domain photograph of a load being strapped or checked
- `images/gallery/gallery-pallet-loading.jpg` — the only suitable public-domain photograph went to the vehicle-loading hero instead, which is more prominent
- `images/gallery/gallery-goods-receiving.jpg` — no public-domain photograph of goods arriving at a warehouse dock
- `images/gallery/gallery-pallet-put-away.jpg` — no public-domain photograph of a forklift working inside a warehouse that did not carry another company's name or phone number
- `images/gallery/gallery-head-office.jpg` — left empty on purpose: it is the /contact hero, beside the company's address, so any office building there would read as 4Jasmine Dr's premises
